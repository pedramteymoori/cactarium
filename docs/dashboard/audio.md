# Audio

The **Audio** tab, `/?tab=audio`, has the volume (JointSpace) and the TV's sound settings (the PHI bridge).

<!-- Screenshot to come: the Audio tab. -->

* **Volume.** A slider, `-` and `+`, and **Mute**, as on the [Control tab](control.md).
* **Sound style.** The TV's presets (Personal, Original, Entertainment, Music, Spatial Music, Dialogue and AI mode on 55PUS8450/12).
* **Clear dialogue**, **Dolby Atmos** and **AI equalizer**. On or off.

Not available while a web app is open: PHI is frozen then, and the sound settings are replaced by a note.
